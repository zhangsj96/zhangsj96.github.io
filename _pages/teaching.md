## Mentoring

Mentoring students in research is one of the most rewarding parts of my work. Many of the students I have worked
with have gone on to Ph.D. programs and postdoctoral positions.

<!-- Interests / philosophy: a few sentences on how you mentor (e.g., student-led papers, weekly meetings, code review). -->

**Stanley Baronett** — Ph.D. student, UNLV (now postdoc, University of Virginia)<br>
_Multi-band radiation hydrodynamics in protoplanetary disks._ Extended the frequency-integrated radiation transport I
developed to multi-frequency thermodynamics of protoplanetary disks. Co-advised with Prof. Zhaohuan Zhu,
Dr. Yan-Fei Jiang, and Prof. Phil Armitage; resulted in
[Baronett et al. (2026)](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2026arXiv260608859B).

**Xiaoyi Ma** — Ph.D. student, KIAA, Peking University<br>
_Thermal response to shadows in protoplanetary disks: the role of competing timescales._ Modeling the thermal
response of millimeter dust continuum and gas line emission to shadows seen in near-infrared scattered light; second
author on a paper submitted to ApJ.

**Bronco Yang** — Undergraduate, Columbia University (now Ph.D. student, Rice University)<br>
_Roles of planet accretion heating on gap opening._ Using Athena++ to study planet–disk interactions including
heating by planet accretion.

**Sarah Harter** — Undergraduate, CSUN (now Ph.D. student, University of Rochester)<br>
_Imaging the dusty substructures due to terrestrial planets with ALMA and the ngVLA._ Co-advised with Prof. Luca
Ricci; published as [Harter et al. (2020), ApJ](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2020ApJ...905...24H).

**Fiona Han** — Undergraduate, University of Michigan<br>
_Synthetic observations of protostellar cores from global simulations._ Co-advised with Prof. Lee Hartmann; presented
at the University of Michigan Astronomy Undergraduate Poster Session.
